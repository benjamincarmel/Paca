M5Stack StickS3 · ESP32-S3

PACA

Cozy purple alpaca virtual pet. Flash the ready-to-go firmware straight from Chrome or Edge — no Arduino IDE required.

Before You Flash

  1. Plug your M5Stack StickS3 into your computer with a USB-C data cable.
  2. Enter Download Mode: press and hold the power button until the internal green LED blinks, then release.
  3. Click Install Paca below, select the StickS3 serial port, and wait ~30 seconds for flashing to finish.
WebSerial is not supported in this browser. Please open this page in desktop Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
WebSerial requires HTTPS (or localhost). Open this flasher from your GitHub Pages URL.
Or download paca-sticks3-merged.bin (flash at 0x0)
